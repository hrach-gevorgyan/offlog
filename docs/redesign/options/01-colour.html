Step 1 · Colour system

Every panel has the same layout, type and content: only the colours change. Each option shows light on the left and dark on the right. The accent appears in only four places: the New task button, the selected item in the sidebar, "Today", and the done check. Red means late, and nothing else. A small dot marks high priority.

The switch at the top adds a muted colour dot for each space. Try it on your favourite option to see whether spaces should carry colour at all. Pick one option, or say "A's neutrals with D's accent", for example.
